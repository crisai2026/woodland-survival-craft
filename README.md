# Woodland Survival Craft

A calm, top-down survival crafting game in the browser, made with a neurodivergent young player:
clean screens, no flashing, nothing loud or sudden, short and literal messages.

## ▶ Play online

**https://crisai2026.github.io/woodland-survival-craft/**

Works in any modern browser on a computer (keyboard needed). The game saves itself in your browser.

## Controls

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| W A S D / arrows | Walk |
| F | Chop, mine, hunt, break (with the rifle or the slingshot: shoot) |
| Space | Jump |
| 1 – 0 | Choose a hotbar slot |
| Q | Place (table, tent, campfire, torch, wall, gate; plant garlic; bed and furnace inside the tent) |
| R (hold) | Eat the food in your hand |
| R | Cook on a campfire, go into your tent or a cave, sleep, reload the rifle, feed a wolf, open a gate |
| E | Inventory and crafting (with a recipe book). Next to the secret treasure chest: open it |
| M | Music on/off |
| H | Show or hide the controls (also Peaceful mode, sounds, forest sounds and volume) |

## What you can do

- Chop trees, mine stone, coal and iron, and craft tools step by step (a Goal box shows what to try next).
- Hunt rabbits, foxes, deer, sheep, moose and bears. Wolves and bears attack, but calmly and fairly.
- Cook meat on a campfire, keep your hearts and hunger up, sleep in your tent at night.
- Walk inside your cozy tent: place your bed and a stone furnace.
- Feed a wolf 3 times (meat or bones) and it becomes your dog.
- Grow garlic, build walls and a wooden farm gate you can lock with a key.
- At night: zombies, skeletons, a werewolf on full-moon nights, and a vampire that only fears garlic
  (use the garlic slingshot!). They all stay out of the light of campfires and torches.
  Peaceful mode turns all attacks off.
- Explore 4 big dark caves (one is a haunted mine): lots of ore, creatures, dripping water,
  and 3 Knight's Quest secrets behind cracked rocks (with Knight's Quest music) — one hides a treasure chest with a trophy coin.
- Soft music, forest sounds and a cozy campfire. And a secret hidden somewhere in the forest...

## Files

- `index.html` — the game page. It loads the game parts from the `js/` folder (no external dependencies).
- `js/` — the game in 19 parts, one per module. All numbers live in `CONFIG` in `js/01-config.js`.
- `SPEC.md` — the game design spec and progress log.
