Controls
W A S D or arrows: walk
F: chop, mine, hunt, break
Space: jump
1 – 0: choose a hotbar slot
Q: place (table, tent, campfire, torch, wall)
R (hold) or right mouse button: eat food
R facing a campfire with raw meat: cook it
R facing your tent: go inside and walk around (place your bed, sleep)
With the rifle: F shoots, R reloads
R facing a wolf with meat: tame it (3 times and it becomes your dog)
E: inventory and crafting
Wolves and bears attack. A moose fights back if you hit it.

M: music on/off
