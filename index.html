Controls
W A S D or arrows: walk
F: chop, mine, hunt, break
Space: jump
1 – 0: choose a hotbar slot
Q: place (table, tent, campfire, torch, wall, gate, furnace, chest, garlic)
R facing a chest (or E): open it and put your things away
R facing a furnace with raw meat or fish: cook it (uses coal)
R facing a gate: open / close it. With the gate key: lock / unlock
R (hold) or right mouse button: eat food
R facing a campfire with raw meat or fish: cook it
R facing your tent: go inside and walk around (place your bed, sleep)
With the rifle: F shoots, R reloads
Axes: wooden, stone, iron (one hit), then the chainsaw (fast on trees; it also hunts and fights)
R facing a cave opening (in the rocks): go inside. R at the light: back out
In caves: lots of ore, hidden treasure chests (E: open) and secrets behind cracked rocks
With the slingshot: F shoots garlic (vampires only fear garlic)
Q with garlic: plant it. F on ripe garlic: harvest
With the fishing rod: face water, F to cast. When a fish bites, F again
R facing a wolf with meat or a bone: tame it (3 times and it becomes your dog)
E: inventory and crafting
Wolves and bears attack. A moose fights back if you hit it.
At night zombies and skeletons come out. On full-moon nights, a werewolf.
Some nights a vampire comes out. Only garlic hurts it.
Night creatures stay out of the light of campfires and torches.

M: music on/off
